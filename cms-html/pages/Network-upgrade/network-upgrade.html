Frequently Asked Questions

Yes 5G users may experience some disruptions like slow internet, dropped calls, or temporary connection issues during the upgrade.

During a network upgrade, it’s normal to have interruptions or unstable connections. This happens because we’re updating equipment and testing new settings to improve the network.These issues are temporary and will lead to a better, more reliable connection once the upgrade is complete.

The site upgrade usually takes around 1 to 3 months, whereby users will be informed upon completion that the network is ready for use.

We’re here to help! Feel free to reach out to YesCare at yescare@yes.my for updates and support regarding any network upgrade issues you're facing.
