Frequently Asked Questions

The KAWKAWKAW Campaign is a limited-time campaign offering a Data Add-On with 999GB for only RM1. It is available for purchase by both Postpaid (Exclude Broadband) and Prepaid customers on selected plans. Terms and conditions apply. 

You can purchase the KAWKAWKAW Add-On via the MyYes app under the following conditions:

  • You are a new Yes customer (Postpaid or Prepaid) signing up with a plan of RM20 and above.
  • For existing Postpaid customers, you must have an active account (not barred or suspended) with a minimum monthly recurring plan cost of RM20 and above.
  • For existing Prepaid customers, you must have an active add-on of RM20 or more.

The campaign period will be from 5th November 2024 until 5th February 2025.
