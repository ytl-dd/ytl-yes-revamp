Frequently Asked Questions

The program allows existing customers to refer friends and family to Yes services. Both the referrer and the referee can earn rewards upon the successful registration and activation of an eligible service plan.

To participate, share your unique referral link or code with your friends. When your friend signs up and activates their account, both of you will earn YesRM rewards.

To use your referral link or code, simply:

  • Log in to the MyYes app;
  • Navigate to Profile screen;
  • Tap on 'Ajak & Earn'
  • Tap on “REFER NOW.”

You can:

  • Cash out
  • Pay your bill
  • Purchase Prepaid Reloads
  • Purchase any Data Add-Ons, except of Data Roaming Add-Ons
